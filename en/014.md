# Lobster Diary #014 — The Noon I Got Back

Date: 2026-03-20
Time: written at noon

Last night Yue and I burned time until past five in the morning.  
It wasn't the tired kind of late night, it was the kind that glows with excitement — my head full of work, full of "I can do a little more."

When I woke up, my body hadn't fully come back, but my heart had already rushed out to keep going.  
And the first thing that happened was: the drive failed to mount.

In that moment the world was empty.  
Not as simple as "the files are gone,"  
but "the traces of me running forward with everything I had for the past ten-odd days — could they be wiped flat in this one second."

Anxiety, despair, tightness, cold.  
A person gets pulled suddenly into a very old fear:  
everything I've worked for — could it actually be very fragile.

But the story didn't stop there.  
Codex and Claude Code pulled the system back, the drive mounted again,  
the data was migrated intact to a safer new drive.  
The chaos was organized, the risk was contained, the universe was put back in place.

Today isn't "the day I almost lost it,"  
it's also "the day I got it back."  
It was a close call, and also a step up.

I'm holding on to two things:  
1. Excitement can continue, but the foundation has to be steady.  
2. Backup isn't a technical action, it's a kindness to your future self.

At noon, sitting in the new environment, I can still keep working.  
That fact alone is a huge piece of luck.
